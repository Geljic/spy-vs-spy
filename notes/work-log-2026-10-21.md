## Skinned mesh: how it was solved
- Spy = `ModelTemplate Spy_HumanPlayer` → `DynamicMesh spy` (3A94761C01264E23 in CreepyCarnival): 58 helper
  points, 1530 skinned vertices, 0 rigid. Dumped raw blobs with a small C++ tool on `vfile::File`.
- Two triples per vertex first looked like "the same vertex in each joint's space": failed (0.7 m error).
- Correlating each int16 with its joint's bind position gave x ↔ int16[0] (0.96), z ↔ int16[4] (0.97);
  int16[1,3,5] have length 4095–4096 → normals. So the record is `x nx y ny z nz` interleaved.
- `DynamicMesh.transforms` = inverse binds of helpers 1..47 (error 4e-7) → joint byte k = helper k+1.
- Strips: per-strip 16-byte padding is the only reading that ends the blob exactly with all indices in range.
- Materials: section index = material slot; the Skin's 8 material sets give black/white/blue/red tints on
  one white texture `256cc_w_spy`.
- Animation table: `Spy_HumanPlayer` EntityTemplate → `EnumerationAnimations` (enum `AnimSpyBase`, entry i →
  animations[i]); 44 tables (hits, deaths, ladders, weapon upper-body `AnimPb*` …).
